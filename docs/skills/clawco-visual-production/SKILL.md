## Asset production workflow (ART-FIRST, small batches)

**Stage 1 — Identity master:** Produce one polished neutral full-body character master on transparent background with front/three-quarter direction consistent with the target, and a turnaround / side-and-back reference as needed for meaningful turns. Retain the large composite only as reference, NOT as a flattened runtime sprite. Obtain owner identity sign-off.

**Stage 2 — Registered modular art:** Author matching high-resolution transparent layers: face skin/head, face expression atlases (eyebrows, eyes/lids, mouth), hair back/front, ponytail, bow, torso/hoodie/skirt, paired upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, shins, socks, shoes/feet, backpack and plush/props as separate interchangeable sprites. Preserve consistent rendering across ALL pieces. Export alpha-correct PNG layers/atlases with a manifest (not only low-detail SVG polygons). Maintain authoring masters or source layers. Each part records source canvas, bbox, origin, pivot (local and global), z-order, intended neighbors, and motion envelope. Registration must be tested by reassembling the exact neutral reference without visible seams.

**Stage 3 — Mechanical fit:** Adapt existing `ArticulatedCustomerRig.gd` and `art/performers/actor_01/rig.json` to the new art. Preserve independent shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle bones and existing tested motion invariants. The animation rig must serve approved art; do not degrade the artwork to fit previous 128×128 sprites.

**Stage 4 — Animation:** First prove foot-planted 60 FPS walk on neutral ground, then anticipation/concentration, near-miss/frustration, cheer/win, real reach to prize hatch, true plush grip/hold, directional turn/exit, idle/talk/watch. Full-body movement includes coordinated hip/torso/head/hair follow-through, gait weight and controlled eye direction. A global bob plus moving feet is insufficient. Fix sliding, snapping, disconnected pivots, dead eyes, clipping bow/hair, implausible prize placement. State changes use authored deterministic inputs; staged choreography is labeled demo only.

**Stage 5 — Arcade composition:** AFTER character and movement gates pass, use approved machine-free architectural plate plus separately registered authentic M01. Floor contact, perspective scale, occlusion and camera angle must be coherent; maintain entrance corridor. NO baked-in patron sprites, fake clickable machines, duplicate charging station, collage of the legacy 4-machine/workshop screenshot, or pasted static customer silhouettes. Hide all rejected old UI/cart in a **focused art proof**, or separately integrate an explicitly approved tactile cart in a later gate. Other machine models in final aisle must be independent assets, never painted into the architecture.

**Stage 6 — Stage presentation:** Bind to deterministic simulation only once art proof is accepted. Separate art-demo win sequence from real authoritative jackpot/stock/financial state. A character visibly collecting a prize from a still art proof is not evidence of actual payout/inventory correctness. Keep all machine collisions/stock and service requests authoritative elsewhere.

## Art-generation guidance (Astra or compatible image model)

Use GPT Astra for illustrated masters and cohesive, registrable part creation. Free Gemini may be used for concept comparisons, not trusted for exact-registration atlases. Give the actual approved target, negative Godot screenshot, and clean environment reference in every relevant art request. **Do not ask for 25 separate vaguely similar character generations in one batch.** Generate coherent, registered groups and verify pivots, seam edges and neutral-pose reconstruction before the next group. Do not claim AI-generated exploded parts are registered until a real Godot assembly test passes.

If a generator cannot preserve exact identity and part alignment, use carefully supervised manual art corrections/source-layer editing. Do not silently replace professional illustrated detail with simple procedurally generated SVGs in the name of developer speed.

## Technical conventions and evidence

- No redesign of core simulation, machine admission, inventory, 60Hz deterministic clock, approved M01 V3.1 X/Y/Z rig, or its masked occlusions.
- `rig.json` remains authoritative for joint position/scale/angle; update schema version with a compatibility loader when new layer metadata is needed. Preserve old technical rig in a fallback **diagnostic** location, not the final gameplay path.
- Import at correct color space with transparent edges; avoid fringe/halos or nearest-neighbor pixellation when painting style calls for smoothing.
- Test in Godot 4.7.2 at **1280×720, 60 FPS true captured output**, without synthetic interpolated frames or claims of 60 FPS based only on encoding metadata. Capture at least a native-resolution still, source-registered neutral still, 1:1 head/torso crop, moving full-body shot, and a relevant environment-context view.
- Preserve a performance record of actual frame delivery / movie duration. A 24 FPS test may be valid as an engineering preview, but cannot be represented as fulfilling 60 FPS acceptance.
- The top-line aesthetic showcase may never include invented `PASS`, commit SHA, GitHub PR, FPS or video-player graphics that imply actual results. Clearly label generated dashboards/mockups `CONCEPT ONLY / NOT GODOT EVIDENCE`.
- Keep screenshots, source reference and reproducible scripts together. Mechanical tests and aesthetic approval are different reports.
